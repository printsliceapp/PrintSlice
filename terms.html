1. The service

PrintSlice is a browser-based tool that splits large STL 3D models into smaller, bed-sized pieces and generates connector geometry (tabs, pins, dovetails) so the printed pieces can be joined back together. Processing happens entirely in your browser — your model files are never uploaded to or stored on our servers.
