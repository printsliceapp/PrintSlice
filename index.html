HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to printable parts

01

Load your STL

Drop in any binary or ASCII STL. PrintSlice reads it straight in your browser — nothing uploads to a server.

02

Set your bed size

Enter your printer's bed dimensions and a safety margin. PrintSlice splits the model into watertight pieces that each fit.

03

Place connectors, export

Click seams to add snap-fit joints, preview the fit in 3D, then download every piece as ready-to-slice STL files.

CONNECTORS

Built from real solid geometry

Rectangular key

A simple straight tab and socket — fast to place, reliable on flat seams.

Pin & socket

A round peg and matching bore — good for smaller or curved surfaces.

Dovetail (locking)

A wedge-shaped joint that resists pulling apart — the strongest of the three.

Your models never leave your computer

Splitting, connector generation, and the 3D preview all run in your browser. No upload wait, no server ever sees your file.
